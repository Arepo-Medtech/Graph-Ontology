## ⚠️ Not routine

- ⚠️ **Routine cholera vaccination of travellers is NOT recommended — the risk to travellers is very low**, even though cholera is endemic in some countries Australians visit. It is for **travellers at high risk of exposure**. [S1]

## Which vaccine

- **Oral inactivated (Dukoral) or live (Vaxchora) — no preference in the immunocompetent.** ⚠️ **The immunocompromised, or anyone on immunosuppressive treatment, must use the oral INACTIVATED vaccine.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Children 2–6: 1 dose of live vaccine, OR 3 doses of inactivated.** [S1]
