## Prevention: aspirin

- **Cancer Council makes a strong recommendation to consider universal aspirin chemoprevention**, except where contraindicated. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Initial aspirin prescription for colorectal cancer prevention is limited to ages 50 to 70.** [S1]
- **Lynch syndrome: the recommended preventive dose is 100 mg daily**, and **regular colonoscopy must continue** while taking aspirin. [S1]

## The symptomatic patient

- ⚠️ **No symptom strongly predicts colorectal cancer, and none rules it out.** [S1]
- **GPs should use simple investigations in primary care: full blood count, iron studies and iFOBT.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Target a maximum of 120 days from first presentation to diagnosis.** [S1]

## After diagnosis

- **Assess for anaemia and iron deficiency as early as possible before surgery.** [S1]
- **Stage III colon cancer: offer 6 months of adjuvant chemotherapy** unless medically unfit. [S1]
- **Stage II: adjuvant chemotherapy is case-by-case, not a standard of care.** [S1]
- **Metastatic disease: test the tumour for RAS mutations** when metastatic disease is diagnosed. [S1]
- **Screen people with advanced colorectal cancer regularly for psychosocial distress.** [S1]
- **Follow-up: colonoscopy 12 months after surgery.** [S1]
