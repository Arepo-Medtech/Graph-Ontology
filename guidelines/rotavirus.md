# Rotavirus

**Scope:** infant rotavirus vaccination — brands, schedule, age limits, intussusception risk.

## Schedule

- **All infants: a course of oral rotavirus vaccine before 6 months**, the first dose **from as early as 6 weeks**. [S1]
- ⚠️ **The course depends on the brand: Rotarix = 2 doses at 2 and 4 months; RotaTeq = 3 doses at 2, 4 and 6 months.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **There are strict upper age limits** — for Rotarix the limit for dose 2 is 24 weeks. [S1]
- **Preterm infants: start at a chronological age of at least 6 weeks if clinically stable.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Complete the full course even after rotavirus gastroenteritis — one infection gives only partial immunity.** [S1]

## ⚠️ Intussusception

- **Rotavirus vaccination is associated with an increased risk of intussusception** — a form of bowel obstruction most often seen in young children. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Contraindicated with a history of intussusception, a congenital abnormality predisposing to it, or severe combined immunodeficiency.** [S1]

## Practicalities

- **No feeding restrictions** before or after either vaccine. **Infants in households with pregnant women can be vaccinated.** [S1]
- **Vaccination significantly reduces rotavirus-specific and all-cause hospital presentations for gastroenteritis.** [S1]
