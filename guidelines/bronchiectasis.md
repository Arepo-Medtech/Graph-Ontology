## Recognise

- **Bronchiectasis means chronic cough and sputum, recurrent chest infections and CT evidence of bronchial dilatation.** It remains under-recognised and undertreated. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Indigenous Australians carry the highest burden:** about 1470 per 100 000 children under 14 years in remote northern communities, and up to 23% of Indigenous adults having chest CT. They die about 20 years earlier than non-Indigenous adults with bronchiectasis. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Diagnose it early in children, where mild disease can reverse.** Over 60% of referred adults had symptoms since childhood and a worse prognosis. Use paediatric radiographic criteria when reading a child's chest CT. [S1]

## Assess

- **The diagnosis starts the work-up:** look for an underlying cause, comorbid conditions and treatable traits, and grade extent and severity. Baseline and extended investigations differ for children and adults. [S1]

## Treat infection

- ⚠️ **Offer eradication therapy for newly detected *P. aeruginosa*** (the 2023 statement changed "consider" to "offer"), since *P. aeruginosa* predicts worse outcomes. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Treat an exacerbation with antibiotics for at least 14 days** (up from ten days in 2015). In children with non-severe exacerbations, oral amoxycillin–clavulanate beat placebo. [S1]
- **Long-term antibiotics (> 2 months) aim to cut bacterial load and airway inflammation** when eradication is not possible. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Consider a long-term oral macrolide in frequent exacerbators (≥ 3 exacerbations a year)**: a trial of at least six months, reviewed regularly for benefit, with monitoring for rare but severe adverse effects. [S1]
- **Do not prescribe inhaled antibiotics routinely.** Consider them in adults chronically infected with *P. aeruginosa* with frequent exacerbations when macrolides are contraindicated, not tolerated or fail. [S1]
- **In children, long-term azithromycin reduced exacerbations most between 17 and 62 weeks after starting** (a newer report, after the statement). [S1]

## Prevent and plan

- **Advise avoiding e-cigarettes** (now named among products to avoid). The statement also details surgery indications and pre-operative work-up, and equitable access to specialist care, including telehealth for rural and remote areas. [S1]
- **Plan transition from paediatric to adult care:** documented, developmentally appropriate and involving both teams. [S1]
