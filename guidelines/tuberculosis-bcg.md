# Tuberculosis (BCG vaccination)

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** who should receive BCG, how it is given, and pre-vaccination tuberculin testing. TB treatment is out of scope.

## Who

- **BCG is recommended for:** ⚠️ **Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children under 5 in some parts of Australia** · **healthcare workers at high risk of exposure** · **young children travelling to high-incidence settings** · **some children of parents from high-incidence countries** · **young children who are household contacts of a person with leprosy**. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people in some states and territories experience a significant burden of tuberculosis.** [S1]
- **BCG is recommended for those at highest risk of severe outcomes.** The WHO considers tuberculosis **a global emergency**. [S1]

## How

- **A single dose by intradermal injection.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **A tuberculin skin test before BCG is recommended for some people**, on a risk assessment of past exposure. **Health professionals must correctly administer and interpret the TST — consult state or territory TB guidelines.** [S1]
