## ⚠️ Recognise it — the features can be non-specific

IMD usually presents as **meningitis and septicaemia**; **septicaemia, alone or with meningitis, can be
particularly severe** [S1]. Features can include [S1]:

**sudden fever · rash (petechial, purpuric or maculopapular) · headache · neck stiffness · photophobia ·
altered consciousness · muscle ache · ⚠️ cold hands · thirst · joint pain · nausea · vomiting**

> ⚠️ **"Not all symptoms or signs may be present"** [S1]. **Prompt diagnosis and treatment of suspected
> cases are critical**, and **IMD is notifiable in every state and territory** [S1].

## Two vaccine families

| | protects against |
|---|---|
| **MenB** | serogroup **B** |
| **MenACWY** (quadrivalent conjugate) — **MenQuadfi, Menveo, Nimenrix** | serogroups **A, C, W, Y** |

*[S1]*

**Anyone from 6 weeks of age who wants to reduce their risk can receive both** [S1].

## Who is funded

- **MenACWY — all children at 12 months**, through the NIP [S1].
- **MenB — Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children under 2**, and **anyone from 2 months with a
  qualifying medical condition** [S1].

## ⚠️ The dose count depends on the brand AND on risk

**Starting MenACWY at 6 weeks to 5 months:**

| | Menveo · Nimenrix | MenQuadfi |
|---|---|---|
| **healthy infant** | **3 doses** — 8 weeks between 1st and 2nd; 3rd at 12 months | **4 doses** |
| ⚠️ **at-risk medical condition** | **4 doses** | **4 doses** |

*[S1]* In all 4-dose schedules the 4th is **at 12 months or 8 weeks after the 3rd, whichever is later**.

> ⚠️ **An at-risk infant on Menveo or Nimenrix needs one more dose than a healthy infant.** Getting the
> table wrong under-immunises exactly the children who most need it.

**Brand:** under 12 months, **Menveo or Nimenrix are preferred**; from 12 months **no preference** [S1].
**Complete a primary course with the same brand where possible** [S1].

## ⚠️ Who is at increased risk

**Complement deficiency** (factor H, factor D, properdin) · ⚠️ **acquired complement deficiency from
complement inhibitors — eculizumab, ravulizumab, pegcetacoplan** · **functional or anatomical asplenia,
including sickle cell disease** · **HIV, regardless of stage or CD4 count** · **haematopoietic stem cell
transplant** [S1].

⚠️ **Anyone starting a complement inhibitor is in this group** — a drug-induced risk easily missed [S1].

**Adolescents and young adults:** cases concentrate at **15–24 years, peaking at 18–20**, and they carry
**the highest rate of meningococcal carriage** [S1]. Higher risk in **close quarters — new military
recruits, students in residential accommodation** [S1].

## Bexsero and fever

- ⚠️ **Children under 2 can have Bexsero separately from other routine vaccines, at least 3 days apart, to
  reduce fever** — routine vaccines first [S1].
- **Paracetamol** is advised when Bexsero is given alongside other scheduled vaccines [S1].
- **MenB and MenACWY can be co-administered at any age** [S1].

## Epidemiology

Vaccination has **reduced serogroup C disease**. **W and Y rose from 2013 and 2015**; ⚠️ **W has fallen
since state MenACWY programs began in 2017** [S1].
