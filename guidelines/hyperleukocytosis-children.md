# Hyperleukocytosis in children

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** recognition, investigation, monitoring and initial supportive treatment of hyperleukocytosis and leukostasis in children with suspected or known leukaemia, pending oncology direction. > ⚠️ **Early oncology consultation, retrieval to a tertiary centre and consideration of PICU for every child with hyperleukocytosis.** Avoid red cells unless oncology directs.

---

## Recognise it

- ⚠️ **Children with high-count leukaemia are at risk of death or serious complications** from leukostasis/hyperviscosity. Hyperleukocytosis should prompt consideration of acute leukaemia. [S1]
- **Leukostasis** (symptomatic hyperleukocytosis) is a clinical diagnosis: respiratory distress, CNS abnormalities or other organ dysfunction attributed to hyperleukocytosis. [S1]
- **Complications:** ischaemic and haemorrhagic stroke · pulmonary or renal thrombosis and infarction · tumour lysis syndrome · DIC. Their frequency depends on the type of leukaemia. [S1]
- **The main risks are bleeding or thrombosis, most often in the brain and lung.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Ask about** visual disturbance, dizziness, tinnitus, behaviour change and altered consciousness. **Look for** altered mental status, ataxia, papilloedema, retinal abnormalities, signs of raised intracranial pressure and increased work of breathing. [S1]

## Escalate

- ⚠️ **Consult oncology early; arrange retrieval to a tertiary centre and consider PICU for all children with hyperleukocytosis.** [S1]
- **Discuss with oncology before further blood collection**, so time-sensitive investigations are prioritised. [S1]

## Investigate and monitor

- **Bloods usually needed:** FBE and film · group and hold · UEC · LFT · CMP · LDH · coagulation screen with fibrinogen · uric acid · G6PD assay. [S1]
- **An adjusted coagulation tube may be needed if haematocrit is under 0.21** — discuss with the laboratory before taking blood. [S1]
- **CXR; consider neuroimaging if there are neurological symptoms or signs.** [S1]
- **Monitoring:** continuous saturations (consider cardiac monitoring) · **4-hourly neuro obs** · **6-hourly FBE, UEC, CMP, uric acid** ± coagulation. [S1]

## Treat

- ⚠️ **Start hyperhydration at 125 mL/m²/hr (or double maintenance) with no added potassium** — 0.9% sodium chloride with 5% dextrose. [S1]
- **Platelet consumption can be extremely rapid; several platelet transfusions may be needed per day.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Correct coagulopathy: keep INR under 2.0, APTT under 80 and fibrinogen over 1.0.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Avoid red cell transfusion unless oncology suggests it, and then limit it to 5–10 mL/kg** to minimise hyperviscosity. [S1]
- **Oncology may recommend allopurinol or rasburicase.** [S1]
