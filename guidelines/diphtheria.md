## Schedule

- **Children: 5 doses at 2, 4, 6 and 18 months and 4 years**, as **DTPa-containing** vaccine. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Never-vaccinated adolescents and adults: 3 doses at least 4 weeks apart, then boosters at 10 and 20 years.** [S1]
- **Rare in Australia but still endemic in many developing countries.** [S1]

## Recognition

- Two presentations: **respiratory or cutaneous.** ⚠️ **Pharyngeal diphtheria forms a greyish or green membrane in the upper respiratory tract**, and was historically the commonest form in unimmunised people. [S1]

## ⚠️ Notify immediately

- **Confirmed or suspected diphtheria should be notified IMMEDIATELY.** **Contacts usually need vaccination (primary or booster) AND prophylactic antibiotics.** [S1]
