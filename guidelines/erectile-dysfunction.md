# Erectile dysfunction

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-22 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** adult men in general practice. ⚠️ **Hypogonadism, Peyronie's disease and ejaculatory disorders
are out of scope** — S1 covers them and they are not the AMH topic closed here.

**AMH topics closed by this guideline (2):** *Erectile dysfunction*, *Impotence*.

## ⚠️ The presentation is a cardiovascular finding

> **It serves as an important marker of future cardiovascular risk, and literature has shown that the risks
> of cardiovascular disease and death increase steadily with the SEVERITY of erectile dysfunction** [S1].
>
> **It is often associated with conditions such as cardiovascular disease, diabetes and hypertension, and
> MAY BE AN INDICATOR FOR UNRECOGNISED DISEASE** [A1].

⚠️ **And the warning that follows from it:**

> ⚠️ **As PDE5 inhibitors can overcome erectile dysfunction due to organic and psychogenic causes, there is
> concern that such symptomatic treatment may lead to CURABLE OR SERIOUS DISEASES NOT BEING
> INVESTIGATED** [A1].

⚠️ **Writing the script without the workup treats the symptom and loses the finding.** **Erectile
dysfunction is more common with increasing age and in those with multiple medical comorbidities, including
metabolic syndrome and chronic medical illness**, and there is **a strong association between erectile
dysfunction and lower urinary tract symptoms/benign prostatic hyperplasia** [S1].

**A third of males will experience one form of male sexual dysfunction in their lifetimes** [S1].

## Assessment

**Determine and treat the underlying cause if possible** [A1]. **Erectile dysfunction is often due to a
combination of organic and psychogenic problems** — **organic causes include neurological, vascular,
endocrine, structural and drug-induced (eg beta-blockers, alcohol misuse)**; **psychogenic may respond to
counselling or behaviour therapy**, and ⚠️ **attention to psychological factors is important in ALL
cases** [A1].

⚠️ **Assess cardiovascular status in relation to the stress induced by sexual activity; for most people this
will be roughly equivalent to mild-to-moderate exercise. An exercise test may be useful** [A1].

**Patient history remains an integral part of evaluation given these dysfunctions represent self-reported
conditions and many lack confirmatory diagnostic tests**, with **emphasis on open dialogue in a
face-to-face consultation and sensitivity towards the patient's unique ethnic, cultural and personal
background** [S1].

## Before the prescription

> **Modifying lifestyle behaviours, managing reversible risk factors and optimising existing medical
> conditions are important FIRST-LINE management options BEFORE pharmacotherapy, and should be performed in
> a holistic, patient-centred approach** [S1].

## PDE5 inhibitors

**The drugs of choice due to their effectiveness and ease of use** [A1]; **the standard of care and
first-line pharmacological intervention** [S1]. ⚠️ **Ineffective in the absence of sexual
stimulation** [A1].

### Doses [S1]

| Drug | Timing | Starting dose | Maximum | Half-life | Food/alcohol |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sildenafil** | **30 minutes before sex** | **25–50 mg** | **100 mg** | **~4 hours** | ⚠️ **absorption affected** |
| **Tadalafil** | **60 minutes before sex** | **10–20 mg** | **20 mg** | **~17.5 hours** | **not affected** |
| **Tadalafil daily** | **daily use** | **5 mg** | — | **~17.5 hours** | **not affected** |
| **Avanafil** | **30 minutes before sex** | **100 mg** | **200 mg** | **~6–17 hours** | — |

**Duration of effect** [A1]: **onset about 30 minutes to 1 hour — avanafil may be effective within 15
minutes.** ⚠️ **Avanafil and sildenafil are short-acting (about 6–8 hours); tadalafil is long-acting (up to
36 hours).**

**Choosing between them:** ⚠️ **comparative trials are lacking**, but **sildenafil and tadalafil appear to
have similar efficacy, including in diabetes or nerve-sparing prostatectomy (limited data for
avanafil)** [A1]. **Most clinical experience has been gained with sildenafil** [A1]. ⚠️ **Tadalafil's
headache lasts 3–8 hours; sildenafil is most likely to cause visual changes; tadalafil back or muscle
pain** [A1].

**All can be used intermittently as needed; tadalafil can also be taken continuously (daily) for erectile
dysfunction OR BPH-related lower urinary tract symptoms** [A1].

### If it does not work [A1]

1. ⚠️ **Is the timing of the dose before intercourse optimal, given the drug's onset and duration?**
2. **Increase to the maximum dose** — ⚠️ **keeping in mind this can cause more adverse effects with limited
   additional efficacy; patients with diabetes or post-prostatectomy are most likely to benefit**
3. **Try an alternative PDE5 inhibitor**

⚠️ **And check testosterone:** **for patients with hypogonadism, restoring testosterone levels will improve
PDE5 inhibitor efficacy and SALVAGE PDE5 NON-RESPONDERS** [S1].

### ⚠️ Nitrates — and the interval before one can be given

> ⚠️ **Contraindicated with nitric oxide donors, organic nitrates or nitrites — including glyceryl
> trinitrate IN ANY FORM, isosorbide mononitrate, isosorbide dinitrate, sodium nitroprusside, amyl nitrite,
> nicorandil — or riociguat, due to risk of profound hypotension or MI** [A1].

**If a nitrate is required AFTER a PDE5 inhibitor has been taken** [A1]:

| Drug taken | Wait before giving a nitrate |
|---|---|
| **Avanafil** | ⚠️ **at least 12 hours** |
| **Sildenafil** | ⚠️ **at least 24 hours** |
| ⚠️ **Tadalafil** | ⚠️ **at least 48 hours** |

⚠️ **A longer period may be required if elimination is delayed (drug interaction, renal or hepatic
impairment). Monitor response carefully** [A1].

⚠️ **This is an emergency-department fact as much as a prescribing one: a man with chest pain who took
tadalafil two days ago is still inside the window.** **Tell your health professional — EVEN IN A MEDICAL
EMERGENCY — that you take this medicine** [A1], and ⚠️ **the interaction can happen even if they are taken
on different days** [A1].

**Alpha-blockers:** ⚠️ **may add to hypotension. If using with an alpha-blocker, STABILISE the alpha-blocker
therapy first, then start the PDE5 inhibitor at the lowest starting dose** [A1] — see the **BPH** guideline,
where the same man is likely to be on one.

### ⚠️ Eyes and ears

**Contraindicated with a history of non-arteritic anterior ischaemic optic neuropathy (NAION)** — and
⚠️ **risk factors for NAION are similar to those for erectile dysfunction** [A1]. ⚠️ **Avoid if there is
significant vision loss in one eye, due to the risk to the other eye** [A1]. **An observational study
suggests an approximate 2-fold increase in risk of acute onset NAION after recent use** [A1].

⚠️ **Avoid in hereditary degenerative retinal disorders (eg retinitis pigmentosa)** — the effect on retinal
phosphodiesterase is unknown [A1].

⚠️ **Rare: sudden hearing loss, which may be associated with tinnitus, vertigo or dizziness** [A1].
**Tell your doctor immediately if you have any sudden loss of hearing or vision** [A1].

### Cardiovascular limits

⚠️ **Contraindicated in men for whom sexual intercourse is inadvisable due to cardiovascular risk factors,
eg unstable angina** [A1]. ⚠️ **There are few or no safety data for men with BP <90/50, uncontrolled
hypertension, stroke or MI within the last 3–6 months, or other severe cardiovascular disease** [A1].

**MI, TIA and stroke, including intracerebral bleeding, have occurred; association with PDE5 inhibitors is
difficult to assess.** ⚠️ **Consider undiagnosed cardiovascular disorders before prescribing** [A1].

**Common adverse effects:** **headache (>10%), dizziness, flushing, dyspepsia, nasal congestion/rhinitis** —
⚠️ **frequency appears dose related**, and **dizziness and dyspepsia are infrequent with avanafil** [A1].

## Second line and beyond

**Intracavernous injection of vasoactive agents such as prostaglandin E1 (alprostadil) is recommended as
second-line therapy in males who did not respond to oral PDE5 inhibitors** — ⚠️ **and the prescription
should include a detailed discussion consisting of technical instruction and education about
priapism** [S1].

**Of the injectable products, alprostadil is least likely to cause penile fibrosis or priapism.**
**Papaverine may be used alone or in combination — consider only if intracavernosal alprostadil is
inappropriate. Phentolamine must be used with papaverine and/or alprostadil to be effective** [A1].

⚠️ **PDE5 inhibitors should be used alone as safety and efficacy are not well established for combination
treatments**, although **some studies suggest combining alprostadil with sildenafil may be helpful** [A1].

**Devices and surgery:** **vacuum constriction devices provide passive engorgement of the corpora
cavernosa, with a constriction ring at the base of the penis to maintain an erection** [S1]. **A penile
prosthesis implant can be offered following appropriate consultation with a urologist** [S1]; **consider
surgery if there is continued failure of drug treatment** [A1].

> ⚠️ **Regenerative therapy — low-intensity shockwave therapy and penile injection of stem cells or
> platelet-rich plasma — IS NOT RECOMMENDED, since these methods lack long-term efficacy and safety records
> and are not properly regulated by the government** [S1].

⚠️ **These are heavily marketed direct to consumers. The statement above is quoted and re-checkable.**

## ⚠️ Priapism

**Explain the treatment of priapism to the patient** before they need it [A1].

| Duration | Action [A1] |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **>2 hours** | **taking two 60 mg pseudoephedrine tablets may help** |
| ⚠️ **>4 hours** | ⚠️ **emergency medical treatment is necessary** |
| **Continued** | **aspiration of 20–50 mL of blood from the corpus cavernosum via a 19 or 21 gauge butterfly needle; may be repeated on the other side** |
| **Still unsuccessful** | ⚠️ **urgent urological review** |

**Dosing principle:** ⚠️ **the ideal dose should produce an erection that lasts NO MORE than 1 hour**, and
⚠️ **a smaller dose is required to maintain adequate erection at home than in the clinic** [A1].

⚠️ **Discourage 'social' use by men who do not have potency problems, since they have a greater risk of
priapism** [A1]. **Regular medical review during the treatment course is necessary** [A1].
