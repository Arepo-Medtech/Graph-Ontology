# Influenza (flu)

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** annual vaccination — who, which vaccine by age, how many doses — plus pregnancy, Guillain-Barré and notification.

## Who

- **Everyone aged 6 months and over is recommended annual influenza vaccine.** [S1]
- **A single annual dose for most people.** Two doses in one season is usually **not recommended, but not contraindicated**. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Two doses, 4 weeks apart, in the first year for:** children 6 months to under 2 receiving it for the first time; children 6 months to under 9 **with a medical risk condition** receiving it for the first time; and **anyone receiving it for the first time after a haematopoietic stem cell or solid organ transplant or CAR T-cell therapy**. [S1]
- ⚠️ **The disease burden is significantly higher among Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people than non-Indigenous Australians in every age group.** [S1]

## Which vaccine, by age

- **Children 2 to under 18** may have either the **intramuscular inactivated** vaccine or the **intranasal live attenuated (LAIV)** vaccine, unless contraindicated. [S1]
- **50 to under 65:** standard-dose **or** adjuvanted. [S1]
- ⚠️ **65 and over: adjuvanted (NIP-funded) or high-dose** — standard-dose only if those are unavailable. [S1]

## Pregnancy

- **Inactivated vaccine at any stage of pregnancy.** Pregnant women should have inactivated rather than live attenuated vaccine — **though there is no evidence of risk from LAIV in pregnancy**. [S1]

## Guillain-Barré syndrome

- **A past GBS episode that was NOT after influenza vaccination carries an extremely low risk of recurrence — vaccination is recommended.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **A GBS episode within 6 weeks of a vaccine may carry a higher recurrence risk** — discuss before vaccinating. [S1]

## Recognition and notification

- Infection ranges from **no or minimal symptoms** to respiratory illness with systemic features, to **multisystem complications and death from primary viral or secondary bacterial pneumonia**. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Influenza is notifiable in every state and territory.** [S1]
