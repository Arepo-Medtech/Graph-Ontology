# Benzodiazepine poisoning in children

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** risk assessment, supportive care, flumazenil and disposition for benzodiazepine overdose in children and adolescents. ## Key points

- ⚠️ **Pure benzodiazepine overdose usually causes mild to moderate CNS depression; deep coma needing ventilation is rare and should prompt a search for other toxins.** [S1]
- **Close observation and supportive care are the mainstay.** **Every intentional overdose needs a mental health assessment.** [S1]
- **Sedation can last up to 36 hours.** Peak levels come 30–90 minutes after ingestion. [S1]
- **Midazolam has a short half-life but active metabolites that accumulate with repeated doses**, prolonging sedation. **Diazepam is long-acting** (half-life over 24 hours) and clears poorly in children with liver disease. [S1]

## Who needs assessment

- **Assess:** every deliberate self-poisoning or significant accidental ingestion, **especially toddlers and younger children**, and any child with CNS impairment. ⚠️ **Consider non-accidental poisoning** if the developmental age doesn't fit. [S1]
- **Always assume the maximum number of missing tablets was taken**, and calculate the maximum possible dose per kg of each drug. [S1]
- **Look for:** confusion, dysarthria, nystagmus, ataxia, hypotonia, seizure · respiratory depression · hypotension · hypothermia · withdrawal or paradoxical reactions (agitation, aggression, hallucinations). [S1]

## Investigations

- **Accidental overdose: usually no tests.** **Intentional overdose: 12-lead ECG, paracetamol level and BSL** for co-ingestants. **Benzodiazepine levels don't help.** [S1]

## Management

- **With respiratory depression, suspect an opioid co-ingestant** — giving parenteral naloxone is reasonable. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Activated charcoal is contraindicated** (aspiration risk). **Enhanced elimination doesn't work** and isn't recommended. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Flumazenil is contraindicated in intentional overdose** — it can precipitate seizures in withdrawal or mixed overdoses. It **may be used in accidental or iatrogenic overdose**, and considered when CNS depression is severe. **Always discuss with a toxicologist** (13 11 26). [S1]
- **Flumazenil: 0.01 mg/kg IV over 15 seconds, max 0.2 mg** (initial dose). [S1]

## Disposition

- **Most isolated ingestions can go home if asymptomatic at 4 hours.** **Admit if still intoxicated beyond 4 hours**; consider admission for every intentional overdose. [S1]
- **Transfer children with CNS depression needing airway protection** — PIPER 1300 137 650. [S1]
- **Discharge: normal GCS after 4 hours' observation and able to walk.** Adolescents with deliberate ingestion need a self-harm risk assessment and a drug and alcohol assessment first. [S1]
