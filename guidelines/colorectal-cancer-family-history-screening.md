# Colorectal cancer — risk and screening by family history

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** assigning colorectal cancer risk category 1, 2 or 3 from family history, and the screening recommended for each. **Not** people with, or related to someone with, a known genetic predisposition (high-risk familial syndromes such as Lynch syndrome). Average-risk population screening is `colorectal-cancer-population-screening`; aspirin chemoprevention in general is `colorectal-cancer-prevention-and-management`. How colonoscopy is performed is the counterpart `colonoscopy` (ACSQHC Colonoscopy Clinical Care Standard).

## Assessing family history

- **Include both sides of the family** when assigning a risk category. [S1]
- **Consider low-dose aspirin 100 mg daily from age 45 to 70** in every category below, in consultation with a health professional. [S1]

## Category 1

- **Who:** near-average risk with **no family history**; **only one first-degree relative diagnosed at 60 or older** also falls here. [S1]
- **Screening: iFOBT in line with population screening.** [S1]

## Category 2

- **Who:** only one first-degree relative diagnosed **before 60**; **or** one first-degree relative **and** one or more second-degree relatives, any age; **or** two first-degree relatives, any age. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Screening: colonoscopy every five years**, starting **10 years younger than the earliest first-degree relative's diagnosis, or at 50, whichever is earlier**, to age 74. [S1]

## Category 3

- **Who:** two first-degree **and** one second-degree relative, at least one diagnosed **before 50**; **or** two first-degree **and** two or more second-degree relatives, any age; **or** three or more first-degree relatives, any age. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Screening: the same five-yearly colonoscopy, but starting at 40 at the latest** (or 10 years before the earliest first-degree diagnosis, if earlier), to age 74 — **and consider referral to a culturally safe family cancer clinic.** [S1]
