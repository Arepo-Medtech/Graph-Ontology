# Camphor poisoning in children

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** camphor ingestion or skin exposure in children (chest rubs, inhalers, mothballs): dose thresholds, seizures, decontamination and disposition. ## Key points

- ⚠️ **Camphor is highly toxic.** Symptoms can begin within 15 minutes of ingesting more than 30 mg/kg; **seizures can occur within 90 minutes.** [S1]
- **Sources:** Vicks inhaler · rubs such as Dencorub, Tiger Balm, emu oils, Vicks VapoRub · some mothballs (others contain naphthalene or paradichlorobenzene instead). [S1]
- **Readily absorbed through skin, but most toxic exposure is by ingestion.** Products may contain other toxins, e.g. **salicylates in muscle rubs.** [S1]
- **The Victorian Poisons Information Centre does not recommend camphor** — no proven health benefit. [S1]

## Time course and dose

- **Onset 5–15 minutes after ingestion; peak levels at 1–3 hours; toxicity typically resolves within 24 hours.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **0.5–1 g is considered a lethal oral dose in children.** [S1]
- **Assess:** all deliberate self-poisoning or significant accidental ingestion · any symptomatic child · **GI irritation and CNS depression over 10 mg/kg; serious toxicity reported over 30 mg/kg.** [S1]
- **Calculate the maximum possible dose per kg** and consider co-ingestants such as paracetamol. [S1]

## Examination and investigations

- ⚠️ **Seizures usually come within 90 minutes**, possibly preceded by fasciculation, confusion, delirium, hallucinations and restlessness. **Respiratory depression is common, often after a seizure.** [S1]
- **A characteristic odour is often present.** Also oral/epigastric burning, nausea, vomiting; case reports of cardiomyopathy and prolonged QRS and QTc. [S1]
- **Investigations:** paracetamol level in all intentional overdoses · ECG · CXR if symptomatic. [S1]

## Management

- **Resuscitate and manage any seizures.** **Wash contaminated skin and remove clothes if indicated.** ⚠️ **Charcoal is not recommended.** [S1]
- **Asymptomatic: observe for 4 hours and discharge if no symptoms develop.** [S1]

## Disposition

- **Consult the local paediatric team if:** intentional overdose · significant or persistent vomiting or diarrhoea · seizures · decreased conscious state or respiratory depression. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Transfer** for persistent seizures or decreased conscious state, or need for respiratory support. **PIPER: 1300 137 650.** [S1]
- **Discharge:** normal conscious state · stable after a symptom-free 4 hours · after deliberate ingestion, low assessed risk of further self-harm. [S1]
