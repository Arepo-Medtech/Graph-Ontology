# SSRI poisoning in children

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** accidental-ingestion thresholds, citalopram/escitalopram cardiac risk, observation and discharge after SSRI overdose in children and young people. ## Who needs assessment

- **SSRI overdose is usually benign, whatever the dose**, and many children and young people stay asymptomatic. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Venlafaxine and desvenlafaxine are SNRIs, NOT SSRIs**: overdose is potentially life-threatening, with delayed seizures, hypotension and cardiac dysrhythmias. [S1]
- **Assess:** every deliberate self-poisoning · any ingestion of unknown quantity · any symptomatic child · any accidental ingestion above the threshold below. [S1]
- **Accidental-ingestion thresholds for assessment:** citalopram >2 mg/kg · escitalopram >1 mg/kg · fluoxetine >5 mg/kg or >60 mg. [S1]
- **…and:** fluvoxamine >10 mg/kg or >100 mg · paroxetine >5 mg/kg or >60 mg · sertraline >10 mg/kg. **These thresholds do not apply to adolescents or adults** — discuss with a toxicologist. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Co-ingested serotonergic agents — MAOIs, tramadol, SNRIs — significantly raise the risk of serotonin syndrome.** [S1]

## Recognise

- **Symptoms usually start within 4 hours and resolve within 12 hours.** Nausea is common; mild serotonin toxicity occurs in under 20%. [S1]
- **Seizures are uncommon (2–4%)** — treat with benzodiazepines. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Citalopram or escitalopram overdose can cause dysrhythmias** — wide-complex bradycardia and torsade de pointes. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Coma or a significantly reduced conscious level is not typical of SSRI overdose** — suspect a co-ingestant or another cause. [S1]

## Investigate and manage

- **Deliberate self-poisoning: 12-lead ECG, blood glucose and paracetamol level.** Large citalopram or escitalopram ingestion: cardiac monitoring and serial ECGs, duration by quantity (discuss with a toxicologist). [S1]
- **Supportive care. Serotonin syndrome: titrated IV benzodiazepines**, with toxicologist advice. [S1]
- **Activated charcoal is rarely indicated** — except that a large citalopram or escitalopram ingestion warrants a toxicologist discussion, as charcoal may be recommended. [S1]

## Observe and discharge

- **Asymptomatic accidental ingestion below threshold: observe at home. Deliberate self-poisoning or above-threshold ingestion: observe 12 hours.** [S1]
- **Symptomatic children usually need only 12–24 hours of supportive care** and can go home once features resolve. Citalopram or escitalopram with an abnormal QTc needs ongoing cardiac monitoring. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Severe serotonin syndrome is managed in PICU.** [S1]
- **Discharge when:** GCS and ECG normal · observation period complete · after deliberate ingestion, a risk assessment shows low risk of further self-harm. [S1]
