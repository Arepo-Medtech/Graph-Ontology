> ⚠️ **Like rubella and unlike measles, mumps vaccine does NOT work after exposure.**

---

## Schedule

- **All children 12 months and over: 2 doses — MMR at 12 months, MMRV at 18 months.** NIP-funded. [S1]

## Recognition

- ⚠️ **Maximum infectiousness is from 2 days before illness onset to 5 days after.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **One-third of infections are asymptomatic.** Symptomatic disease ranges from **mild upper respiratory symptoms to widespread systemic involvement**. [S1]

## Cases

- ⚠️ **Mumps vaccine does not protect people if given after exposure.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Mumps is notifiable in every state and territory.** [S1]
