## Key points

- **A toxidrome is a classic cluster of symptoms and signs** — a 'clinical fingerprint' — and recognising it **focuses the differential** on the few chemicals with similar effects. [S1]
- **Manage each condition under its specific guideline.** For 24-hour advice, call the Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26. [S1]

## The agitated or confused child

- **Anticholinergic:** delirium, hallucinations, agitation · dilated pupils · dry flushed skin, urinary retention. **Agents:** sedating antihistamines (promethazine, doxylamine, cyproheptadine, pheniramine, alimemazine), TCAs, atropine, hyoscine, antispasmodics, atypical antipsychotics, plants (Angel's Trumpet). **Antidotes: physostigmine; sodium bicarbonate for TCAs.** [S1]
- **Hallucinogen:** raised temperature, heart rate and BP · hallucinations, synaesthesia, agitation · dilated pupils · nystagmus. **Agents:** LSD, mescaline, psilocybe mushrooms. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Neuroleptic malignant syndrome:** raised temperature, heart rate and respiratory rate · confusion · dilated pupils · **muscle rigidity**, sweating, metabolic acidosis, liver and renal failure, hyperkalaemia, rhabdomyolysis, clots. **Agents:** antipsychotics and antiemetics (domperidone, droperidol, metoclopramide, promethazine). **Antidote: dantrolene** (reduces rigidity). [S1]
- **Sympathomimetic:** raised temperature, heart rate, BP and respiratory rate · agitation, hypervigilance, paranoia · dilated pupils · sweating, tremor, hyperreflexia, **seizures**. **Agents:** cocaine, amphetamine, pseudoephedrine, nicotine, caffeine, cold and flu medicines. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Serotonin toxicity:** raised temperature, heart rate, BP and respiratory rate · confusion, agitation, coma · dilated pupils · tremor, **myoclonus**, sweating, **hyperreflexia**, trismus, rigidity. **Agents:** MAOIs, SSRIs, tramadol, tapentadol, MDMA, amphetamines, lamotrigine. [S1]
- **Withdrawal from ethanol or sedative-hypnotics:** raised temperature, heart rate, BP and respiratory rate · agitated confusion · dilated pupils · sweating, diarrhoea, tremor, **seizure**. **Agents:** alcohol, benzodiazepines, barbiturates. [S1]

## The sedated or confused child

- ⚠️ **Opioid:** low temperature, heart rate, BP and respiratory rate · CNS depression, coma · **constricted pupils** · hyporeflexia, pulmonary oedema. **Agents:** heroin, morphine, methadone, fentanyl, oxycodone, codeine-derivative cough syrups. **Antidote: naloxone.** [S1]
- **Sedative-hypnotic / ethanol:** CNS depression, confusion, coma · constricted pupils · hyporeflexia. **Agents:** alcohol, benzodiazepines, barbiturates, GHB. **Antidote: flumazenil (benzodiazepines).** [S1]
- **Cholinergic:** confusion, coma · constricted pupils · salivation, lacrimation, urination, diarrhoea, vomiting. **Agents include organophosphates and nerve agents.** [S1]

## Disposition

- **Consider admission for every intentional overdose.** **Transfer** when care must escalate beyond the hospital and local paediatric team. [S1]
- **Before discharge, adolescents with deliberate ingestion need a self-harm risk assessment and a drug and alcohol assessment**, and linkage to services if needs are found. [S1]
