## Who

- **Recommended for: military personnel · laboratory workers who routinely handle *Salmonella* Typhi · travellers aged 2 and over going to typhoid-endemic regions.** [S1]

## Two vaccines

- **Injectable — Typhim Vi: 1 dose IM**, registered from **age 2**. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Oral — Vivotif (live attenuated): 1 capsule on days 1, 3 and 5 — and day 7 on a 4-dose schedule — taken 1 hour before food.** Registered from **age 6**. **4 doses may protect better than 3.** [S1]
