# Vancomycin dosing and monitoring in children

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** IV vancomycin dosing regimens, therapeutic drug monitoring, dose adjustment, administration and infusion reactions in infants and children with normal renal function. Counterpart: `empiric-antimicrobials-children` (the RCH antimicrobial table, which gives the empiric vancomycin dose and a different trough target). ## Principles

- ⚠️ **Infuse intermittent doses IV over at least 1 hour** — rapid infusion can cause a vancomycin infusion reaction. [S1]
- ⚠️ **This guideline assumes normal renal function.** Seek pharmacist or specialist advice for impaired renal function, ECMO or renal replacement therapy. [S1]
- **Take every vancomycin level (trough or random) only at steady state — at least 24 hours after starting.** Earlier levels can lead to wrong dose adjustments. [S1]
- **Target exposure: AUC/MIC 400–650 mg/L.h.** Trough alone is not always an accurate correlate of AUC; AUC targeting has been shown to reduce nephrotoxicity. [S1]

## Choosing the regimen

- **0–90 days: continuous infusion recommended**, target steady-state concentration 17–25 mg/L. If a continuous infusion cannot be used (e.g. limited IV access), dose intermittently with the online calculator, target AUC 400–650 mg/L.h. [S1]
- **Over 90 days: intermittent dosing recommended**, target steady-state trough 8–15 mg/L. Consider continuous infusion (target 17–25 mg/L) in critically ill children or when targets cannot be reached — seek specialist advice. [S1]

## Infants 0–90 days: continuous infusion

- **Loading dose 15 mg/kg IV over 1 hour, then infuse by creatinine (micromol/L) and corrected gestational age:** creatinine <40 and CGA ≥40 weeks **50 mg/kg/day** · creatinine <40 and CGA <40 weeks **40 mg/kg/day** · creatinine 40–60 **30 mg/kg/day** · creatinine >60 **20 mg/kg/day**. [S1]
- **Switching from intermittent to continuous:** start at the previous 24-hour total daily dose, immediately after the last intermittent dose — **no loading dose.** [S1]
- **Level at 24–36 hours** after starting (target 17–25 mg/L), **from a different access point to the infusion**; if in range, repeat 24 hours later. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Out of range: adjusted dose (mg/day) = last maintenance dose × (target ÷ last concentration).** Do not exceed **80 mg/kg/day** without specialist advice. [S1]

## Infants 0–90 days: intermittent dosing

- **Dose and monitor with the KidsCalc calculators** (intermittent-dosing target AUC 500 mg/L.h), using PMA, weight and creatinine. ⚠️ **No loading dose** — loading doses have been shown to increase the risk of renal impairment in this age group. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Do not use KidsCalc — seek specialist advice — for** PMA under 25 weeks, weight under 500 g, renal impairment, ECMO or renal replacement therapy. [S1]
- **First steady-state trough by interval:** 6-hourly before the 5th dose · 8-hourly before the 4th · 12-hourly before the 3rd · 18- or 24-hourly before the 2nd. Take it about 30 minutes before the dose is due, and calculate the AUC each time. [S1]
- **With normal renal function, give the next dose on time** without waiting for the result. [S1]
- **Do not use the AUC calculator** if doses were missed in the prior 24 hours or the level was taken before steady state (under 24 hours after starting or changing the dose) — ask a pharmacist or specialist. [S1]
- **Worked example, AUC over 650:** cease the order and check creatinine, repeat the trough at the next due dose, and restart at a lower adjusted dose once it has fallen below 15 mg/L — **the dosing interval does not change.** [S1]

## Children over 90 days

- ⚠️ **Usual starting dose 15 mg/kg (max 750 mg) IV 6-hourly.** Keep the interval 6-hourly — the only interval where the trough accurately reflects AUC. [S1]
- **Dose on actual body weight, including in obesity, up to the maximum.** Seek specialist advice for initial dosing in moderate to severe renal impairment. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Target steady-state trough 8–15 mg/L, first taken before the 5th dose** (24 hours into 6-hourly dosing), about 30 minutes before the dose is due. This correlates with AUC 400–650 mg/L.h. [S1]
- **Continuous infusion over 90 days (specialist advice):** loading dose 20 mg/kg over 1 hour, then usually **60 mg/kg/day**; level about 24 hours after starting, target 17–25 mg/L. Max 80 mg/kg/day without specialist advice. [S1]

## Administration and adverse effects

- **Dilute to 5 mg/mL or weaker and infuse over at least 60 minutes (max 10 mg/minute).** Up to 10 mg/mL via a central line if needed, with a higher reaction risk. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Nephrotoxic and ototoxic**, especially with other nephrotoxic or ototoxic drugs (e.g. aminoglycosides) and in renal impairment. [S1]
- **Infusion reaction** (formerly 'red man syndrome'): upper-body and neck flushing or rash, chest and back muscle spasm, fever, hypotension, itch — usually settles within an hour. [S1]
- ⚠️ **If a reaction occurs: stop the infusion**, check dose and rate, wait for resolution, dilute further if possible and resume slower; document it. **Give later doses over 90–120 minutes** and consider an antihistamine beforehand. [S1]
- **Consult the paediatric team** for renal impairment, or when AUC or trough cannot be brought into range. [S1]
