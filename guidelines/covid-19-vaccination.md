## Children

- ⚠️ **Healthy infants, children and adolescents under 18 are NOT recommended COVID-19 vaccine** — their risk of severe illness was very low through the pandemic, and **benefits are not considered to outweigh the potential risks**. [S1]

## Older adults

- ⚠️ **All adults 75 and over, including aged care residents: a single primary dose, then further doses every 6 months.** **Risk of severe illness rises significantly with age.** [S1]
- **Further doses every 6 months on a risk-benefit assessment** — most likely to benefit **people with medical risk conditions and residential care residents**. [S1]

## Medical risk and pregnancy

- **People with conditions that raise the risk of severe illness are recommended further doses**, varying by **age and severe immunocompromise**. [S1]
- **Previously vaccinated pregnant women are not routinely recommended a further dose**, but can consider one for risk conditions or preference — without risk conditions, their **risk of severe illness and pregnancy complications from Omicron is very low**. [S1]
