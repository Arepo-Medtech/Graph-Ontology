# Japanese encephalitis

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** JE vaccination — within Australia's JE risk regions, the Torres Strait, laboratory workers and travellers.

> ✅ **OPEN AND QUOTED.** 3 of 3 claims quoted verbatim from the **Australian Immunisation Handbook**; **3 fragments re-checked by machine. 3 quoted doses.**

> ⚠️ **JE is now a domestic Australian risk, not only a travel one.** Risk regions **are set by states and territories and change** as virus is detected.

---

## Who

- ⚠️ **Anyone aged 2 months and over who lives or works in, or will travel to, a JE risk region of Australia AND may be bitten by mosquitoes.** Risk regions **are determined by states and territories and may change**. [S1]
- Also: **laboratory workers exposed to JE virus** · **travellers to endemic areas outside Australia in the transmission season** · **people who live or work on the outer islands of Torres Strait**. [S1]

## Two vaccines

- **Imojev (live) and JEspect (inactivated) — both safe and effective.** ⚠️ **Imojev is contraindicated in pregnancy and in the immunocompromised, and is not registered under 9 months.** People at ongoing risk **may need boosters**. [S1]
