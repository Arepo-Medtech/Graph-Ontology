# Colorectal cancer — population screening

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** organised bowel cancer screening of asymptomatic adults at average risk: the test, eligible ages and interval, and follow-up of a positive iFOBT. **Not** family-history risk (see `colorectal-cancer-family-history-screening`), and **not** prevention, symptomatic referral or management after diagnosis (see `colorectal-cancer-prevention-and-management`). How the colonoscopy itself is done — consent, bowel preparation, sedation, credentialing, reporting — is the counterpart `colonoscopy` (ACSQHC Colonoscopy Clinical Care Standard), which defers indications and intervals to Cancer Council.

## Test, ages and interval

- **Screen with an immunochemical faecal occult blood test (iFOBT) every two years from age 45 to 74.** [S1]
- **Starting population screening at 40 is not recommended**, and **nor is extending the upper age from 74 to 79 or 84.** [S1]
- **Flexible sigmoidoscopy is not recommended as a primary screening test**. [S1]
- **On request, outside the program:** clinicians could consider offering an iFOBT to **fit, well and healthy people aged 75–85**, and to **people aged 40–44 every two years** until their first National Bowel Cancer Screening Program invitation. [S1]

## Who should not be in the program

- ⚠️ **Screening is not for people with symptoms** — rectal bleeding, persistent change in bowel habit, iron-deficiency anaemia — **nor for anyone who should be having colonoscopic surveillance or screening.** [S1]

## After a positive iFOBT

- ⚠️ **Every positive iFOBT needs follow-up investigation**; the sole exception is a clear breach of the sample collection protocol, when **repeat iFOBT within six weeks** is suggested. [S1]
- **Follow-up is a high-quality diagnostic colonoscopy.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Colonoscopy should be done within 120 days** of the positive iFOBT. [S1]
- **If that colonoscopy needs no further colonoscopic follow-up, the participant skips the next round** of program iFOBT screening. [S1]
