# Rubella

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** rubella vaccination, women of child-bearing age, men born 1966–1990s, and why the vaccine is not post-exposure prophylaxis.

> ✅ **OPEN AND QUOTED.** 6 of 6 claims quoted verbatim from the **Australian Immunisation Handbook**; **7 fragments re-checked by machine. 4 quoted doses.**

> ⚠️ **Unlike measles, rubella vaccine does NOT work after exposure.** See [`measles`](measles.md), where MMR within 72 hours **is** post-exposure prophylaxis.

---

## Schedule

- **All children 12 months and over: 2 doses — MMR at 12 months, MMRV at 18 months.** NIP-funded. [S1]
- **Adolescents and adults born during or since 1966 without 2 doses** — especially **healthcare workers, childhood educators and carers**, and **women of child-bearing age who are seronegative**. [S1]

## ⚠️ The two groups to check

- **Women of child-bearing age who are seronegative should be vaccinated** — the purpose is preventing **congenital rubella syndrome**. [S1]
- ⚠️ **All males born during or after 1966 — particularly 1966 to the 1990s — should have their records reviewed for 2 MMR doses.** **They are more likely than females to have missed them.** [S1]

## Cases

- ⚠️ **Rubella vaccine does not protect people if given after exposure.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Rubella is notifiable in every state and territory.** [S1]
