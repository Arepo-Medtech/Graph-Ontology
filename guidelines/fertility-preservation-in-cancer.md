# Fertility preservation in people with cancer — key points

## Discussing the risk

- **Discuss the impact of cancer treatment on fertility with all women diagnosed with cancer.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Serum AMH does not predict oocyte quality or the chance of conception.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Tell young patients their fertility status is likely uncertain and that they need contraception.** [S1]

## Males

- **Cryopreserve any sample that contains viable sperm, however few.** [S1]
- **In mid and late-pubertal boys with a testicular volume of 8 ml or more, sperm can be harvested under general anaesthesia by testicular sperm extraction.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Testicular biopsy for fertility preservation in pre-pubertal boys remains unproven.** [S1]

## Females

- ⚠️ **Oocyte stimulation and retrieval take ten to fourteen days, so early referral should be a high priority.** [S1]
- **Ovarian tissue cryopreservation is the main option for pre-pubertal girls.** [S1]
- **Goserelin is PBS-listed for premenopausal women receiving an alkylating agent for any malignancy.** [S1]

## Contraception (Society of Family Planning guidance, as reported)

- ⚠️ **Women with active cancer should avoid combined hormonal contraception because of the risk of DVT.** [S1]
- **The copper IUD is recommended as first-line contraception for women with breast cancer.** [S1]
