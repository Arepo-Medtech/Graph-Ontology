## Certification

- ⚠️ **Yellow fever vaccination and the ICVP certificate are given only at state- or territory-approved Yellow Fever Vaccination Centres.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **The certificate becomes valid 10 days after vaccination.** Arrivals without a valid ICVP are told to **seek medical assessment if symptoms develop within 6 days** of leaving a yellow fever-declared place. [S1]

## ⚠️ Adults 60 and over

- **Adults 60 and over have a higher risk of severe adverse events** — neurotropic and viscerotropic. **They should be vaccinated only if they intend to travel to endemic countries.** [S1]

## Duration and laboratory workers

- **One dose gives lifelong immunity in most healthy adults and children** — protective antibody in **75–100% measured 10–69 years after vaccination**. [S1]
- **Laboratory workers: 1 dose**, and **with ongoing exposure, measure neutralising antibody if the last dose was 10 or more years ago**. [S1]
