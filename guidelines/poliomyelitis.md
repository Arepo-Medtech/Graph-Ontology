# Poliomyelitis

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** IPV vaccination for children and travellers, and notification.

> ✅ **OPEN AND QUOTED.** 3 of 3 claims quoted verbatim from the **Australian Immunisation Handbook**; **3 fragments re-checked by machine. 2 quoted doses.**

---

## Schedule

- ⚠️ **Children: 4 doses of IPV-containing vaccine at 2, 4 and 6 months and 4 years** — **not at 18 months**, unlike DTPa and Hib. **First dose from 6 weeks.** [S1]

## Travellers

- ⚠️ **Travellers to areas or countries with polio cases: an IPV booster every 10 years.** [S1]

## Notification

- ⚠️ **Polio is notifiable in every state and territory.** [S1]
