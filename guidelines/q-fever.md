## Who

- **Q fever vaccine is recommended from age 15 for people at risk of *Coxiella burnetii* infection who have not had previous Q fever infection or vaccination.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **At-risk occupations:** abattoir workers · farmers · stockyard workers · shearers · transporters of cattle, camels, sheep, goats and kangaroos · vets, vet nurses and students · professional dog and cat breeders · pet food workers · agricultural college staff and students · wildlife and zoo workers with kangaroos and bandicoots · animal refuge and shelter workers · people who cull or process kangaroos or camels · laboratory workers with veterinary specimens. [S1]

## ⚠️ Screen first

- **Pre-vaccination screening is required.** The Q-Vax product information says it is for **adults**; **ATAGI says it can be used from age 15**. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Q fever is notifiable in every state and territory.** [S1]
