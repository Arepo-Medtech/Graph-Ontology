# Haemophilus influenzae type b (Hib)

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** Hib vaccination for infants, catch-up, asplenia and stem cell transplant.

> ✅ **OPEN AND QUOTED.** 4 of 4 claims quoted verbatim from the **Australian Immunisation Handbook**; **4 fragments re-checked by machine. 3 quoted doses.**

---

## Schedule

- **Infants: 4 doses at 2, 4, 6 and 18 months.** The **first can be as early as 6 weeks**; if so, the next doses stay at **4 and 6 months**. [S1]
- **Children under 5 who missed a dose** should catch up. [S1]

## ⚠️ Immunocompromise

- ⚠️ **Asplenia (functional or anatomical): 1 dose of Hib.** ⚠️ **Haematopoietic stem cell transplant: 3 doses, starting 6 months after the transplant.** [S1]

## Disease

- Hib causes invasive disease including **epiglottitis, septic arthritis and cellulitis**. [S1]
