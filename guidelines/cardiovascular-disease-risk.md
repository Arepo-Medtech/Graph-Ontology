# Cardiovascular disease risk — assessment and management

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-21 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** adults **without known CVD**. People with established CVD are outside this guideline.

> **This is the first guideline in this set whose primary source is Australian.** The 2023 Australian
> Guideline for assessing and managing cardiovascular disease risk [S1] is a Commonwealth publication
> (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0), developed by the Heart Foundation with the Australian Chronic Disease Prevention
> Alliance and **endorsed by the RACGP**. Where the other guidelines borrow international guidance and bolt
> on a PBS layer, this one is local throughout.
>
> Filed under "hypertension" in the coverage matrix, but that framing is now wrong: Australian practice
> treats blood pressure within **absolute risk**, not as a number to be treated in isolation.

## Treatment aims

Reduce the probability of a cardiovascular event over the next 5 years. The unit of decision is the
**risk estimate**, not any single risk factor. The guideline explicitly prefers "risk estimate" over
"absolute risk" because the calculator output considers multiple factors specific to the person [S1].

## 1 · Who to assess

| Group | Assess from |
|---|---|
| All people without known CVD | **45–79 years** (conditional) [S1] |
| People with diabetes, without known CVD | **35–79 years** (conditional) [S1] |
| First Nations people, without known CVD | **30–79 years** using the calculator (consensus) [S1] |
| First Nations people 18–29 | assess **individual risk factors** (consensus) [S1] |

## 2 · Who is high risk without calculating

Do **not** use the calculator in people already known to be high risk. Assess as high risk by consensus
recommendation [S1]:

- **Moderate-to-severe chronic kidney disease** meeting any of: sustained eGFR <45 mL/min/1.73m²; men with
  persistent uACR >25 mg/mmol; women with persistent uACR >35 mg/mmol
- **Confirmed familial hypercholesterolaemia**

## 3 · Risk categories and management

Estimated 5-year risk of a cardiovascular event [S1]:

| Category | 5-year risk | Management | Reassessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High** | **≥10%** | Healthy lifestyle **plus prescribe** blood pressure-lowering and lipid-modifying pharmacotherapy | Formal reassessment not generally required; manage and follow up clinically |
| **Intermediate** | **5% to <10%** | Healthy lifestyle; **consider** BP-lowering and lipid-modifying pharmacotherapy depending on clinical context | Every 2 years if not on pharmacotherapy; sooner if near the high threshold or risk factors worsen |
| **Low** | **<5%** | Healthy lifestyle; pharmacotherapy **not routinely recommended** | Every 5 years; sooner if near the intermediate threshold |

**For First Nations people, reassess every year** as part of an annual health check or opportunistically, or
at least every 2 years — in both the intermediate and low categories [S1].

Pharmacotherapy is qualified throughout as "unless contraindicated or clinically inappropriate, and in
discussion with the person on the benefits and harms of treatment", encouraging shared decision-making [S1].

## 4 · The calculator

The **Aus CVD Risk Calculator** is based on the **PREDICT-1° equation**, developed from a large New Zealand
population cohort and **modified and recalibrated for the Australian population and health setting** [S1].

Inputs: age, sex, smoking status, total cholesterol : HDL-C ratio, diabetes status, CVD medicines, postcode,
history of atrial fibrillation. For people with diabetes it additionally uses HbA1c, time since diagnosis,
uACR, eGFR, BMI and insulin use in the previous 6 months [S1].

It can be used in people already receiving BP-lowering, lipid-modifying or antithrombotic therapy, because
it includes a variable accounting for treatment [S1].

**The new categories are not interchangeable with the 2012 Framingham-based equation categories.** The
guideline states this explicitly; the percentile ranges for high, intermediate and low differ [S1]. Anyone
carrying forward a risk category recorded before this guideline is carrying forward a number on a different
scale.

## 5 · Reclassification

Factors that may move an estimate up or down, particularly near a category threshold: **ethnicity, eGFR and
uACR, severe mental illness, coronary artery calcium score, and family history of premature CVD** [S1].

Two definitions the guideline pins down, because both are otherwise applied loosely [S1]:

- **Family history of premature CVD** — coronary heart disease or stroke in a first-degree **female** relative
  aged <65, or a first-degree **male** relative aged <55.
- **Severe mental illness** — a current or recent mental health condition requiring specialist treatment,
  whether or not received, in the 5 years before assessment.

Also addressed as risk considerations: ankle-brachial index, high-sensitivity CRP, chronic inflammatory
conditions including rheumatoid arthritis, **history of pregnancy complications** (hypertensive disorders of
pregnancy including pre-eclampsia, and gestational diabetes), premature or early menopause, lipids,
polygenic risk scores and COVID-19 [S1].

## Australian context — PBS

PBS Schedule 4333, checked directly [S2]:

| Class | Agents | Benefit type |
|---|---|---|
| ACE inhibitors | perindopril, ramipril | **unrestricted** (also R) |
| ARBs | irbesartan, telmisartan, candesartan | **unrestricted** (also R) |
| Calcium channel blockers | amlodipine | **unrestricted** (also R) |
| Diuretics | indapamide, hydrochlorothiazide | **unrestricted** (also R) |
| Beta blockers | metoprolol | **unrestricted** (also R) |
| Statins | atorvastatin, rosuvastatin, simvastatin | **unrestricted** (also R) |
| Ezetimibe | — | **unrestricted** (also R) |
| PCSK9 inhibitors | evolocumab | authority required (streamlined) |
| **Alirocumab** | — | **not on Schedule 4333** |
| siRNA | inclisiran | authority required (streamlined) |

**This is the opposite pattern to the rest of the set.** Every agent the guideline names for first-line
management — BP-lowering and lipid-modifying — is unrestricted. Where migraine, insomnia and GAD push the
prescriber away from the best-supported agent, here the subsidy and the guideline agree. The constrained
agents (PCSK9 inhibitors, inclisiran) sit beyond the first-line recommendations.
