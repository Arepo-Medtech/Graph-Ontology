# High-risk low-dose ingestions in children

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-verified; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** the drugs and chemicals that can seriously harm a small child after one or two dose units or a mouthful, their toxic features, and the rule that any suspected ingestion is admitted for observation. General principles are in `poisoning-initial-management-children`; agent pages include `tricyclic-antidepressant-poisoning-children`, `recreational-drug-overdose-children` and `foreign-body-ingestion-children` (lead).

> ✅ **OPEN AND QUOTED.** 15 of 15 claims quoted verbatim from the **RCH Melbourne Clinical Practice Guideline *High risk - low dose paediatric ingestions*** (undated page); **15 fragments re-checked by machine. 0 quoted doses.**

> ⚠️ **One or two tablets, or a mouthful, can kill a toddler.** **Admission for observation is mandated if there is any concern** a small child has taken one of these agents. **Discuss every case** with a senior clinician, Poisons Information (13 11 26) or a toxicologist.

## Why these agents matter

- **Most accidental paediatric ingestions are well below a toxic dose**, but ⚠️ **a handful of agents are dangerous after 1 or 2 dose units or a mouthful** and demand a cautious risk assessment. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Admit for observation if there is any concern that a small child may have ingested one of these.** **Discuss all cases** with a senior clinician, Poisons Information or a clinical toxicologist. PIPER: 1300 137 650. [S1]

## Prescription drugs

- **Amphetamines:** agitation, hypertension, hyperthermia, seizures. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Calcium channel blockers:** bradycardia, hypotension, refractory shock — **toxicity may be delayed beyond 12 hours with sustained-release preparations.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Chloroquine and hydroxychloroquine:** rapid-onset coma, seizures, cardiovascular collapse. [S1]
- **Opioids (methadone most toxic):** coma, respiratory depression. [S1]
- **Propranolol and sotalol** (other beta blockers are better tolerated): coma, seizures, ventricular dysrhythmias, hypoglycaemia. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Sulfonylureas: hypoglycaemia, which may be delayed 8 hours.** [S1]
- **Theophylline:** seizures, SVT. **Tricyclic antidepressants:** coma, seizures, hypotension, ventricular tachycardia. [S1]

## Illicit drugs

- **Amphetamines (ice, speed, ecstasy):** as for prescription amphetamines, plus **hyponatraemia with ecstasy.** [S1]
- **GHB:** rapid-onset respiratory depression and coma, recovering within a few hours. [S1]

## Chemicals

- ⚠️ **Camphor:** rapid-onset coma, seizures, hypotension. **Hydrocarbons, solvents and eucalyptus oil:** rapid-onset coma, seizures and aspiration. [S1]
- **Lead foreign body (eg fishing sinker):** abdominal pain, nausea, **delayed encephalopathy**, seizures. [S1]
- **Naphthalene moth balls:** methaemoglobinaemia, haemolysis. Most moth balls now contain less toxic paradichlorbenzene. [S1]
- **Organophosphate and carbamate insecticides:** cholinergic syndrome, seizures, coma. ⚠️ **Paraquat herbicide:** oropharyngeal burns, multi-organ failure, pulmonary fibrosis. [S1]
