## Suppression

> ⚠️ **The routine use of dopaminergic drugs — cabergoline and bromocriptine — is NOT recommended for
> suppressing physiological lactation** [M1].
>
> **They may be used to prevent onset of lactation in the puerperium for clearly defined medical
> reasons.** **Cabergoline is preferred as it has fewer side effects than bromocriptine** [M1].

**Conservative measures** used to relieve discomfort from breast engorgement [M1]:

- **Breast support**
- **Expression of small amounts of milk — only enough to reduce pain**
- **Ice packs**
- **Paracetamol**
- **Application of bruised cabbage leaves to breasts**

⚠️ **AMH states evidence of their efficacy is limited** [M1].

---

## Stimulation

⚠️ **Measures such as breastfeeding more frequently and ensuring correct positioning and attachment are
preferred** [M1].

> ⚠️ **The dopamine antagonists domperidone and metoclopramide have been used to stimulate lactation,
> however evidence is limited and there are increasing safety concerns about both drugs** [M1].
>
> **AMH names no specific safety concern, no dose and no alternative drug.**
