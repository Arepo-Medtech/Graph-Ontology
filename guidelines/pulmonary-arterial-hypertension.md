## ⚠️ The co-prescribing rules — read these first

These are the claims a general practice most needs, because the prescription that causes the harm is
usually written somewhere else.

> ⚠️ **RIOCIGUAT IS CONTRAINDICATED WITH:** **nitrates** (e.g. glyceryl trinitrate), ⚠️ **NICORANDIL**,
> **PDE5 inhibitors** (sildenafil, tadalafil), and **other phosphodiesterase inhibitors** — **anagrelide,
> dipyridamole, milrinone, theophylline** [P3].

> ⚠️ **SILDENAFIL FOR PAH IS CONTRAINDICATED WITH:** a **nitrate**, **riociguat**, and **ritonavir, oral
> ketoconazole or itraconazole** [P4].

⚠️ **This closes a loop with two other guidelines in this compendium.** `angina` prescribes **nitrates**
and **nicorandil**; `erectile-dysfunction` prescribes **sildenafil and tadalafil**. A patient on riociguat
who presents with chest pain and is handed a GTN spray, or who is prescribed a PDE5 inhibitor for erectile
dysfunction, meets a contraindication head-on.

> ⚠️ **AND IT CONSTRAINS THE TREATMENT LADDER ITSELF.** AMH says combinations are used when monotherapy is
> inadequate — **and notes in the same breath that riociguat is contraindicated with PDE5 inhibitors**
> [P1]. **Riociguat cannot simply be added to a sildenafil or tadalafil regimen.**

## What treatment is for [P1]

**Anticoagulants, digoxin, diuretics and oxygen may be required. Specific treatments improve symptoms and
quality of life, and may increase survival** [P1].

**Drug choice is based on severity and cause, response to treatment, clinical experience and patient
preference** [P1].

| Option [P1] | |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ **High-dose calcium channel blockers** | only for patients who **respond favourably to an acute vasodilator challenge** — ⚠️ **FEWER THAN 10% will respond to, or tolerate, them long term** |
| **Endothelin antagonist** | **ambrisentan, bosentan, macitentan** |
| **PDE5 inhibitor** | **sildenafil, tadalafil** |
| **Prostacyclin** | **selexipag, inhaled iloprost** |
| **sGC stimulator** | **riociguat** |
| ⚠️ **Continuous IV epoprostenol** | **recommended for class IV at highest risk**; **an RCT and some observational studies show it increases the survival rate** |

> ⚠️ **THERE ARE NO LONG-TERM SURVIVAL DATA FOR THE OTHER PROSTACYCLINS** [P1]. The oral and inhaled
> agents **improve haemodynamics and avoid IV administration** [P1] — which is not the same claim.

**Two practice points** [P1]:
- **Consider pulmonary veno-occlusive disease if pulmonary oedema occurs during vasodilator treatment.**
- ⚠️ **Infections, particularly respiratory, can have serious consequences in PAH — consider immunisation
  against influenza and pneumococcal disease.**

## Endothelin antagonists [P2]

**Endothelin-1 has a pathogenic role in pulmonary hypertension, promoting vasoconstriction, cell
proliferation and vascular remodelling; the antagonists block endothelin receptors, causing vasodilation
and improving exercise capacity** [P2].

> ⚠️ **CONTRAINDICATED BY THE MANUFACTURERS when baseline aminotransferases exceed 3 × ULN** [P2].

**Bosentan has the MOST drug interactions and appears MOST LIKELY to cause hepatic dysfunction.** **The
agents differ in selectivity for endothelin receptor types, but there is no evidence this is clinically
important** [P2].

| Hepatic limits [P2] | |
|---|---|
| **Ambrisentan, macitentan** | **contraindicated in severe impairment (no data); not recommended in moderate** |
| **Bosentan** | **cautiously in mild; contraindicated in moderate-to-severe, Child-Pugh B or C (no data)** |

> ⚠️ **CONTRAINDICATED IN PREGNANCY, teratogenic in animals.** **Ensure effective contraception during
> treatment AND FOR AT LEAST 3 MONTHS AFTER STOPPING** [P2].

**May worsen fluid overload and anaemia; may cause pulmonary oedema in pulmonary veno-occlusive
disease** [P2].
**Common: reduced haemoglobin, flushing, peripheral oedema (may be less with macitentan), headache, nasal
congestion (may be dose related), nasopharyngitis or sinusitis, abdominal pain (uncommon with
bosentan)** [P2].

| Monitoring [P2] | |
|---|---|
| **Haemoglobin** | **baseline, 1 month, 3 months, then every 3 months** |
| **Counselling** | ⚠️ **report nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, fatigue, DARK URINE or JAUNDICE immediately** |

## Riociguat [P3]

**A soluble guanylate cyclase stimulator targeting the nitric oxide pathway. Indicated for idiopathic,
heritable, connective-tissue-disease- or congenital-heart-disease-associated PAH, and for chronic
thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension after surgery or when inoperable** [P3].

### ⚠️ Two absolute bars beyond the drug interactions

> ⚠️ **CONTRAINDICATED in pulmonary hypertension associated with IDIOPATHIC INTERSTITIAL PNEUMONIAS** — a
> trial showed **INCREASED MORTALITY** and serious adverse effects including respiratory disease and lung
> infections compared with placebo [P3].

> ⚠️ **RIOCIGUAT INCREASES THE RISK OF SERIOUS AND FATAL BLEEDING, including respiratory tract bleeding.**
> **Avoid after serious haemoptysis or bronchial arterial embolisation surgery** [P3].

### Dose [P3]

| | |
|---|---|
| **Start** | **0.5–1 mg three times daily for 2 weeks** |
| **Titrate** | **increase by 0.5 mg three times daily every 2 weeks if no hypotension and systolic BP >95 mmHg** |
| **Maximum** | **2.5 mg three times daily** |
| **If hypotension** | **decrease by 0.5 mg three times daily** |
| ⚠️ **Not recommended** | **systolic BP <95 mmHg** |

> ⚠️ **IF TREATMENT IS INTERRUPTED FOR 3 DAYS OR MORE, RESTART AND RE-TITRATE AS IF STARTING FOR THE FIRST
> TIME** [P3]. A patient who misses a long weekend of doses cannot resume at their old dose.

> ⚠️ **SMOKING CHANGES THE DOSE.** **Tobacco smoking decreases riociguat concentrations, and the dose may
> need to be adjusted BY AS MUCH AS 50% if the patient stops or starts smoking** [P3]. **Counsel the
> patient to report either.** A successful quit attempt is a dose event.

**Renal and hepatic:** **CrCl <80 mL/min — start lowest dose, monitor for hypotension especially during
titration; not recommended if CrCl <15 mL/min. Moderate hepatic impairment — lowest dose, monitor BP; not
recommended in severe impairment** [P3]. **The elderly are at increased risk of hypotension — lowest dose,
monitor BP** [P3].

⚠️ **Contraindicated in pregnancy, teratogenic in animals — effective contraception during and FOR AT
LEAST 1 MONTH AFTER STOPPING. Breastfeeding contraindicated** [P3].

**Before starting: check BP, renal function and haemoglobin.** **Avoid antacids within 1 hour of a dose**
[P3]. **May be used as monotherapy or with an endothelin antagonist or a prostacyclin** [P3].
**PBS-listed for both CTEPH and PAH; tablets 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 and 2.5 mg** [P3].

## Sildenafil for PAH [P4]

> ⚠️ **THE PAH DOSE IS NOT THE ERECTILE DYSFUNCTION DOSE.**
> **PAH: 20 mg THREE TIMES A DAY** [P4] — a standing, scheduled dose. The `erectile-dysfunction` guideline
> in this compendium carries a different, as-required regimen from a different AMH monograph.
> ⚠️ **Same molecule, different monograph, different indication, different dose. Do not carry one across.**

| | [P4] |
|---|---|
| **Usual** | **20 mg three times a day** |
| **If poorly tolerated** | **consider reducing to 20 mg twice daily** |
| **Higher doses** | **some people may require them — specialist supervision** |
| **IV substitution** | **the 20 mg oral dose can be substituted with 10 mg IV** ⚠️ **conversion based on LIMITED pharmacokinetic data; NO data for higher doses** |

> ⚠️ **RAPID CLINICAL DETERIORATION MAY OCCUR WHEN SILDENAFIL IS STOPPED — reduce the dose gradually**
> [P4]. This is not a drug a patient can simply run out of.

- ⚠️ **In PAH secondary to SICKLE CELL DISEASE, sildenafil increases the risk of serious adverse effects
  such as vaso-occlusive crises requiring hospitalisation, and limited data suggest it does NOT improve
  exercise capacity** [P4].
- ⚠️ **The FDA has warned that HIGH DOSES were associated with INCREASED MORTALITY in a long-term
  dose-ranging trial in CHILDREN with PAH** — while **sildenafil is approved for this purpose in the
  European Union** [P4]. **Two regulators, two positions, both recorded.**
- **Nosebleeds are common**, especially with concurrent oral anticoagulants or where the pulmonary
  hypertension is secondary to connective tissue disease [P4].
- **Prolonged erections and priapism have occurred; immediate medical treatment is necessary if an erection
  lasts more than 4 hours** [P4].

**What the class actually buys:** **PDE5 inhibitors increase exercise capacity in pulmonary hypertension.**
⚠️ **Further data are needed on how long the effects last, whether they improve WHO functional class and
survival, and whether subgroups respond differently** [P4].
