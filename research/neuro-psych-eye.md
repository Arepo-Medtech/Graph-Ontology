## 4. Findings and licence surprises

- **Consensus quota:** the signed-in Consensus account ran out mid-task: it read 0 searches left after the first clusters and -9 by the end. The quota resets on October 1. I ran six `country: au` searches in total: SMA, MG and spasticity without `open_access`, then MG and nAMD/RVO with `open_access: true`, plus uveitis/glaucoma/keratitis and narcolepsy/RLS. Remaining clusters fell back to Europe PMC and Crossref, as the rules allow. The international second pass (no `country`) was not run on Consensus.
- **RANZCP has withdrawn its own CPGs.** The mood disorders guideline (2020), the anxiety/panic guideline (2018) and the schizophrenia guideline (2016) are removed from the RANZCP library. RANZCP labels them "reference use only" because they exceed the NHMRC five-year lifespan, and the 2016 PDF carries a watermark to that effect. RANZCP's antipsychotics-in-BPSD guideline is "rescinded pending review". The only current Australian psychiatry guideline in scope is the 2026 ANZJP GRADE schizophrenia guideline.
- **No ANZAN clinical guidance exists.** The public ANZAN site has no guidelines or position statements section. ESA's positions page has no status epilepticus guideline. MS Australia was not needed because the group has no MS rows. The Australian MND guideline is only "halfway" (FightMND, 26 May 2026), and its site returned a WordPress error today. Macular Disease Foundation Australia offers education, not clinical guidance. The Australasian Neuromuscular Network points to the international 2007 SMA consensus.
- **AI-use bans:** AAO (all PPPs and EyeWiki) says content "may not be reproduced, copied, or put into any artificial intelligence program ... without permission". ESC requires a licence for "generative AI models". This may block even AI-assisted paraphrase. NICE and APA content is © with no reuse outside the UK or US-fair-use without written agreement, so it can only be paraphrased.
- **Stroke Foundation living guidelines:** "No part of this publication can be reproduced by any process without permission", so paraphrase only, although the spasticity chapter is current.
- **No-derivatives licences:** these are widespread (EAN ALS, MGFA 2020, EAN narcolepsy, RMSANZ BoNT statement, TSC 2021, APVRS CMV, Cure SMA 2024). ND arguably bars adapted text even with attribution.
- **Retired or superseded international sources:** the AAN botulinum toxin guideline (blepharospasm/cervical dystonia/spasticity) was retired on 2025-04-07; Dressler 2021 (CC BY) is the open replacement. The NHMRC 2010 glaucoma guideline is rescinded, and RANZCO's glaucoma collaborative-care CPG is past its 2022 review date.
- **Probable triage mis-mappings:** Bronchospasm, Analgesia or fever, Panic disorder, OCD, Mixed episodes and palliative-care rows sit under Neurology (ATC-derived). ATTR-CM and hATTR sit under Ophthalmology.
- **Scope gaps:** No usable source was found anywhere for **Corneal grafts** (rejection prophylaxis). **Gyrate atrophy** has only a 2026 case series (CC BY) and EyeWiki. **Chronic spasticity** (weight 18; oral baclofen/dantrolene/diazepam) has no Australian source; NICE CG145 covers children only. **Terminal disease** (bromazepam, flunitrazepam) is not covered by any source found.
