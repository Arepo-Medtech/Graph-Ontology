## 5. Licence surprises, dropped rows, method notes

- **RCH is stricter than the rules assume.** RCH Terms of Use cl. 5.2, as read, give a personal-use licence only and bar copying, reproducing or republishing without RCH's prior written consent. RESEARCH_RULES gives RCH as the example of `quote_with_attribution`, but here it is classed `limited`. This affects every RCH-based guideline already in the project. The owner should decide.
- **HGSA/ASIEM:** the site terms prohibit all reproduction (personal non-commercial use only), and every handbook PDF carries an all-rights-reserved notice. Classed `paraphrase_only` and flagged non-commercial.
- **health.gov.au (LSDP Fabry, NBS):** the site copyright page forbids any commercial use. Classed `non_commercial`. Services Australia, by contrast, is CC BY 4.0.
- **Living Evidence diabetes guideline:** non-commercial, and the PDF covers only T1D devices, not basal insulin choice.
- **Springer Nature consensus statements** (prolactinoma, acromegaly outcomes, PH1) are free to read but the publisher holds exclusive rights and there is no open licence.
- **Not verifiable (`unknown`):** Wiley, OUP, Karger, Elsevier and J Hepatol pages returned 403 or a Cloudflare challenge. Where Europe PMC metadata suggested a licence, it is recorded in `notes` as a lead only. RANZCO (403) and Children's Health Queensland (403 via curl, WebFetch and Firecrawl) could not be assessed, so the CHQ metabolic/hypoglycaemia pages are NOT included. SCHN has no public metabolic emergency protocol pages (only service and lab pages were found).
- **Consensus tool:** 3 AU-filtered searches were run (pituitary; PKU/IEM; paediatric endocrine). The first parallel attempts were rate-limited, then the monthly quota ran out ("-3 searches left"), so the remaining clusters were searched with WebSearch and Europe PMC instead. Consensus surfaced the ESA male hypogonadism statement, the APEG paediatric T2D guideline (not a gap row here) and ISPAD 2022. It found no Australian pituitary guideline.
- **Dropped (now `admin` in triage):** *Ablation of thyroid remnant tissue*: no AU source; international is ATA 2025 DTC guideline (doi 10.1177/10507256251390877, licence not read). *Above pressure injury*: AU is QUT 'Nutrition and Wound Healing' summary 2024 (© QUT) and the NPIAP/EPUAP/PPPIA International Guideline 2026, which is free to download and quotable only with the exact statement plus its strength.
- **Mis-binned rows:** Osteomyelitis, Keloid, Lichen planus hypertrophic, Lichen simplex chronicus, Necrobiosis lipoidica, Granulomata, Hypsarrhythmia, the DMO/PDR rows and Local intra-articular infiltration landed in Endocrinology via ATC (H01/H02/S01/J01).
