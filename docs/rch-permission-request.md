# RCH Melbourne: permission request (draft, NOT sent)

**Status:** drafted 2026-09-23 for the repository owner to send. Claude has not sent it.

## Why
The RCH Terms and Conditions (https://www.rch.org.au/terms-and-conditions/, read 2026-09-23), clause 5.2, grant *"a revocable
licence to download, copy and print the content on the Site for your personal use"*. Otherwise users *"must not … reproduce,
republish … or disclose or distribute any part of this Site (or any content on it) in any way, except as otherwise expressly
stated on the Site or with the RCH's prior written consent"*.

**216 guidelines in this compendium quote RCH Clinical Practice Guidelines verbatim.** They were authored on the footing
"© RCH, free to read, quoted with attribution", which was **set without reading clause 5.2**. That footing was an error, recorded
here and in `docs/no-guideline-action-agenda.md`. Nothing has been removed. Whether fair dealing covers the current use, whether
to seek consent, and whether to restrict distribution meanwhile are decisions for the owner (and possibly legal advice).

**Subject:** Request for written consent to quote RCH Clinical Practice Guidelines, with attribution

Dear RCH Clinical Practice Guidelines team,

We maintain the Australian Medicines Compendium. It is a verification-first reference that links Australian treatment guidance
to PBS medicines and SNOMED CT-AU. Every clinical statement is quoted word for word from its source, with a citation, the
guideline's "last updated" date and a link, so any reader can check it against your current page.

Under clause 5.2 of your Terms and Conditions, we are writing to request your prior written consent to reproduce excerpts from
the RCH Clinical Practice Guidelines in this way. Specifically:
- short verbatim excerpts: recommendations, doses and criteria. Never whole pages, images or flowcharts;
- each excerpt carrying the RCH copyright notice, the guideline title and date, and a link to the live page;
- a prominent statement that the RCH page is authoritative and the excerpt may be out of date;
- no use of RCH branding or logos.

We would update or withdraw any excerpt on request, and we are happy to share the full list of guidelines and excerpts.
