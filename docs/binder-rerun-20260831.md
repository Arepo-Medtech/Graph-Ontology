# Binder re-run at `20260831` — what the bump actually cost

**2026-09-23.** Pin bumped `20260731` → `20260831`, binder re-run over all 639 conditions,
`apply_corrections.py` re-applied. Previous state is recoverable from git at commit `54bf981`.

## ⚠️ I understated the cost of the bump, and the re-run showed it

I reported the bump as **"one display changed, nothing retired"**, from validating all 301 candidate
codes against both editions. **That measurement was sound but the conclusion drawn from it was too
narrow.**

It tested whether each **code still resolves** and whether its **display string** matched. It did not —
and could not — test whether the repo's **binding rule** still fires. The rule is *exact or normalised
match on display **or designation***, and **August changed designations that my code-level check never
looked at.**

**Every one of the affected codes validated perfectly, both before and after.**

## What was lost

**4 condition rows across 3 concepts lost their binding:**

| condition | concept | bound via | why it broke |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-small cell lung cancer | `254637007` | `exact_display` | display renamed **cancer → carcinoma** |
| Differentiated thyroid cancer | `1255086008` | `exact_synonym` | display was already *carcinoma*; the matching **synonym was dropped** |
| Triple-negative breast cancer | `706970001` | `exact_synonym` | display unchanged (*Triple negative malignant neoplasm of breast*); **synonym dropped** |
| Triple negative breast cancer | `706970001` | `normalised_synonym` | as above |

> ⚠️ **These bindings were not wrong, and the concepts have not changed meaning.** `254637007` is still
> non-small cell lung cancer. **SNOMED renamed things; the lexical rule stopped matching.** The binder
> behaved correctly per `binding_policy_v2` — which is exactly why they are now `candidate_unconfirmed`
> and not silently re-bound.
>
> **They are four strong candidates for the review worksheet**, and a person confirming them is cheap.
> Nothing here re-binds them, because that is a terminology judgement.

## ⚠️ A gap in the "improving the binder never discards a review" guarantee

**15 rows carried `method: candidates_removed` before the re-run. They came back as
`candidate_unconfirmed` with candidates restored, and `apply_corrections.py` did not recover them —
because none of the 15 is in `binding_corrections.json`.**

`candidates_removed` is written by **no script in this repository**. It exists only in the data file. So
some curation step — most likely the quarantine pass that produced `snomed_candidates_review.json` —
recorded work in a form the corrections mechanism does not cover.

**The guarantee covers *corrections*, not *candidate removals*.** That is a real hole, and this re-run
is what exposed it.

**Nothing was fabricated to paper over it.** Recording those 15 as `rejected` would have put a decision
in the ledger that no person made, and `candidates_removed` does not mean `rejected`. They are listed in
git at `54bf981` and can be re-stripped or properly recorded once someone decides which they are.
