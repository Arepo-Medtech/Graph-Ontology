# Cancer Council Australia: permission request (draft, NOT sent)

**Status:** drafted 2026-09-23 for the repository owner to send. Claude has not sent it.

**Why:** Cancer Council's guideline FAQ allows fair dealing with correct citation. It asks that anyone reproducing
"more than 200 words or figures/tables" contact **guidelines@cancer.org.au**. The COSA guidelines hosted on the same
site (fertility preservation; head and neck nutrition) use **cosa@cancer.org.au**.

Until permission is granted, every guideline authored from these sources:
- quotes at most 200 words per source guideline, enforced by `cc_gen.py`;
- quotes nothing from a table, because tables are stripped from the retrieved text before quoting;
- leaves everything else out rather than paraphrasing it.

In several guidelines (cancer pain, melanoma, teleoncology, fertility preservation) every formal recommendation and
dose is in a table, so those pages hold practice points only.

---

**To:** guidelines@cancer.org.au  (cc: cosa@cancer.org.au for the COSA guidelines)
**Subject:** Request to reproduce excerpts of Cancer Council clinical practice guidelines, with attribution

Dear Cancer Council Australia guidelines team,

We maintain the Australian Medicines Compendium, a verification-first reference that links Australian treatment
guidance to PBS medicines and SNOMED CT-AU. Every clinical statement in it is quoted verbatim from its source,
with a citation and locator, so a reader or a machine can re-check it against the original.

We would like permission to reproduce excerpts from the following guidelines, beyond the 200-word fair-dealing
guidance, including the recommendation tables (recommendation text, strength and practice points, including drug
doses where given):

- Cancer pain management in adults
- National cervical screening clinical guidelines
- Colorectal cancer (population screening; risk and screening based on family history; the parent guideline)
- Keratinocyte cancer
- Melanoma
- Hepatocellular carcinoma surveillance
- Neuroendocrine neoplasms
- Early detection of cancer in adolescents and young adults
- COSA: fertility preservation; nutritional management of head and neck cancer

Each excerpt would carry:
- full attribution: guideline title, version, publication date and MAGICapp link;
- the date retrieved;
- a note that the current version is authoritative.

We would not reproduce figures or images. We would update or withdraw any excerpt on request.

Could you let us know whether this is acceptable, and on what terms?
